## Launching Your First Shopify Store

1. Click the blue button above to open the official Shopify site and start a trial or paid plan.  
2. Choose a store name, set your region, and configure basic business details in the admin.  
3. Add products with images, variants, pricing, and inventory settings.  
4. Pick a Shopify theme, customize your homepage and navigation, and connect your domain.  
5. Install Shopify apps from the app store shopify listing pages for reviews, shipping, or marketing as needed.  
6. Enable payments, test checkout, and publish your Shopify website when you are ready to sell.

## Resolving Common Admin and Storefront Issues

- Checkout not loading? Confirm payment providers are active and your domain DNS points correctly.  
- Theme changes not visible? Clear browser cache and verify you published the correct Shopify theme.  
- App conflicts? Disable recent Shopify apps one at a time and retest the storefront.  
- API errors? Check credentials, scopes, and rate limits in your partner or admin settings.  
- Mobile display problems? Test responsive settings and review any Shopify store to mobile app wrapper configuration.
